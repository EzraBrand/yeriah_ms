## Notes
- Only two red-barred lemmas appear clearly on this page (lines 14–15 and 32); line 9 `והרוח` and lines 10–11 repeat the lemma of 84a:39–40 (`והרוח אינה יכולה לנצח אותה ונאחזת על גבורה`), apparently written in plain ink here.
- 1–4: the incense atones for those "cut off before their time", whose death comes from the measure of Cain who killed Abel; accusers attack the spirit so that it does not rise to its source (`מחצבה`). "As one exacts payment from the transgressors, so from the …" (line 4–5; `הנעכרים[?]` recheck).
- 5–6: `עולה השמימה` echoes Judg. 13:20 / the incense smoke rising.
- 7–9: `כמנין ימות החמה` (84a:39) explained as a notarikon of three verses; the words `שעה[?] מנחה` and the three abbreviations `לש' ולת"ת ולשכ'` are uncertain (probably: to the Name, to Tiferet, to Shekhinah). Interlinear additions near `כמנין` and at the line end; recheck.
- 11–12: Abraham, `איש החסד`; Chesed "shortens the life of the body, lengthening the soul".
- 14–15: lemma `שעשה … שעקדת יצחק חלום היה ולא דבר אחר` — the Yeri'ah calls the Binding "a dream"; the commentary reads it as incense-like "thought" that atones for the slain. `אא"ע[?]` = `אברהם אבינו ע"ה`[?].
- 17: liturgy `ועקדת יצחק היום לזרעו תזכור` (Rosh Hashanah Zikhronot).
- 18: Ex. 30:38 `איש אשר יעשה כמוה … ונכרת מעמיו`; 20: Gen. 22:1; 21: Gen. 22:12; 21–22: Gen. 22:13 `והנה איל אחר נאחז בסבך`.
- 24–31: the incense is "thought", not a complete deed; a priest offered it only once (B. Yoma 26a, "no one ever repeated it"). 30–31: Job 40:19 `ראשית דרכי אל העושו יגש חרבו`; Ex. 17:11 `וכאשר ירים … וכאשר יניח`. Reading `ל הטושו[?]` = `אל העושו`.
- 32: lemma `כי לא לרצון יהיה צמר ופשתים` — cf. Gen. 4:4–5 (Abel's wool offering accepted, Cain's flax rejected; the midrash on *sha'atnez*, Pirkei de-R. Eliezer 21). Abel = the righteous line (incense of Aaron), Cain = Korah's incense (Num. 16).
- 35: interlinear letter above `בזה`; recheck.
- 37: `החזקה[?]` uncertain.
- 41: `אב"ר[?]` — probably an epithet of Abraham (אב רם?); recheck. Text continues on 85a.
