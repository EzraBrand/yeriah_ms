## Notes
- 1–6: Ps 15:2. תמים = Binah (mercy), צדק = Chokhmah (judgment, as 76b). "Walking" ascribed to Binah, "acting" to Chokhmah; ascent from below.
- 6–21: Gen 2:13 + Gen 10:8. Third river = Pachad (Gevurah). גיחון ← Gen 3:14 על גחונך תלך. כוש = blackness = sin. נמרוד / עשו suckle from Pachad. 16: Gen 27:3 וצודה לי ציד. 17–21: Ra'avad (Pseudo-Ra'avad on Sefer Yetzirah?) on Isaac/Abraham and right/left; Chesed suckles from Chokhmah (judgment), Gevurah from Binah (mercy).
- 22–25: Gen 2:14 פרת; פרים ורבים; Gen 36:1. Fourth river = Pachad again? (MS reads הפח'; check against 76b where the four rivers are ח'ב'ג' + ?). עשו = הנחש הקדמוני.
- 26–34: Tree of Life = middle line (Tiferet) = ישראל; Gen 32:25–29; Jacob "at the heel of the chariot" before the fight; Michael = Chesed side, releases the angel = Samael. Cf. Bereshit Rabbah 78:1.
- 35–42: Tree of Knowledge = בת יעקב = Malkhut; Prov 30:21–23; the Shekhinah fights Samael until "dawn" = the hidden light (אור הגנוז). Lemma 8b: הנאשם ונעלם דרך הנחש עלי צור ← Prov 30:19.
