## Notes
- Numbering: kraken numbers 03–47 are kept; crop `Main_NN` = kraken NN+2. 03 and 42 are interlinear words (`Main_01_F`, `Main_40_F`), not text lines.
- Red rubric marks on this folio sit in the interline space directly **above** the first words of a lemma (clear at 28 `כי השלשלת` / `בצלמו`, where the bar climbs over the lamed, and at 31 `והירך` / `ממקומו`), not under them. The `red` flags in `lines.tsv` therefore mark the line above the lemma as well as the lemma line. Marked words: 10 `אחר התרדמה כי`, 11 `יתבוששו`, 21 `כי בצלם`, 22 `אלהים ברא`, 28 `כי השלשלת`, `בצלמו`, 29 `ותחסרהו מעט`, 31 `והירך`, `ממקומו`, 35 `ותאכל ותתן`, 36–37 `לאשה`, `חרבך`, 43 `מצות עשה`, 45 `והדרך`, `תעשה`. 45 `והדרך` also has a dotted line under it.
- 03–10: end of lemma 8b (77a:41) `הנאשם ונעלם דרך הנחש עלי צור` ← Prov 30:19. צור = הע' (Atarah / Malkhut); the serpent's זוהמא (BT Shabbat 146a) fights Jacob as it did Adam. 06–07: BT Bava Batra 58a שופריה דיעקב מעין שופריה דאדם הראשון. 08: Gen 2:21 ויקח אחת מצלעותיו; Gen 32:32 והוא צולע; 09: Gen 2:21 ויסגור בשר; 10: Gen 32:27 כי אם ברכתני. Limping = hardness of judgment; closing of flesh / blessing = its softening.
- 10–25: Yeri'ah lemma `אחר התרדמה כי ממנו יצא המתגבר ולא יתבוששו` (Gen 2:21, 2:25). Darkness and תרדמה = absence of the intellectual powers, dominance of the bodily ones; in Jacob the dark power of שרו של עשו left after the night, in Adam the hardness of judgment left after the sleep, but Jacob keeps limping while in Adam the limp was only during the taking of the rib. המתגבר = the wife from the rib, "opposite him" (BT Yevamot 63a זכה עזר לא זכה כנגדו). 21–25: before the sin they were "as the pattern above" (Gen 1:27 בצלם אלהים … זכר ונקבה ברא אותם), the lower ones stirring only with the upper; no shame because no gaze, and gaze belongs to מה' (Malkhut).
- 26–27: the moon not yet diminished (BT Chullin 60b), "no facing and receiving" — Malkhut equal to Tiferet.
- 28: `כי השלשלת היתה כפולה בצלמו` — gloss: twins were born (Bereshit Rabbah 22:2), as above.
- 29–34: `ותחסרהו מעט` ← Ps 8:6. After the serpent's זוהמא the chain is no longer double; diminution from the hidden light called שמים. `והירך נשמט ממקומו` (cf. Gen 32:26 ותקע כף ירך) = Hod, the left thigh, displaced from Pachad (Gevurah); the serpent entered the Pardes, the rib touched the thigh = beauty of Adam and of Jacob, the shining face withdrew.
- 35–36: `ותאכל ותתן גם לאשה` ← Gen 3:6 (לאישה); Eve followed material desires from the primeval serpent and "cut the shoots" (BT Chagigah 14b קיצץ בנטיעות).
- 36–42: `חגור חרבך על ירך` ← Ps 45:4; joined to the midrash סחטה ענבים ונתנה לו (Bereshit Rabbah 19:5). חרבך = Pachad, source of desire; ירך = Hod; the wine she pressed is emanated from there.
- 43–46: `גבור הודך והדרך` (Ps 45:4) read as Gevurah + Hod; `מצות עשה` = פרו ורבו (Gen 1:28, 9:1) spoken "for no good intent"; `והדרך צלח` ← Ps 45:5 = לא תעשה, "no desire in it at all", on the right side, whence the emanation of man. Last word of 46 cut at the edge; catchword `מורה` per kraken.
- Uncertain: 03 interlinear word (`ונעלם`?), 07 `לשם`, 14 `מא'`, 18 `עת`, 27 `הככר לדפ'`, 28 `בצלמו פי' תאומים`, 45 `לא`.
