## Notes
- 1–5: end of the 77b discussion. Man = form (צורה), woman = matter (חומר); the matter is drawn from מה' (Malkhut). Line 4 is
  damaged by a struck-out word; the lemma at its end reads רכב יסוד (?) and lines 5 quotes the Yeri'ah's words המעבר / המכריע
  (Yesod as the "decider" between matter and form). והערה in 4 may be ועטרה (Malkhut).
- 6–13: Gen 3:7 ויתפרו עלה תאנה. תאנה ↔ תואנה (pretext, cf. Judg 14:4); the "leaf" = lower world, the "fruit" = upper world =
  the (Active) Intellect; ויתפרו = "they went deep" into that attribute and cut the shoots (קצץ בנטיעות, Chagigah 14b).
  13: נאצלת / אנושית are red-underlined (Yeri'ah's words: the human form emanates from there).
- 14–18: Prov 27:18 ונוצר תאנה יאכל פריה. Guarding oneself from the leaves of the tree = the powers of judgment; "eating the
  fruit" = the intellect that flows from the hidden light (אור הגנוז). 16: ראש לשועל — perhaps read ראש לשל מעלה ("chief over
  what is above"); cf. Avot 4:15.
- 18–22: Yeri'ah: ותהי איבה בין האריה והדוב (איבה ← Gen 3:15; lion = Chesed, bear = Pachad/Gevurah). "The world runs its
  course" (Avodah Zarah 54b) when the shining face withdraws; Prov 30:21–23 quoted again (as 77a:37–38).
- 21–27: the generation of the flood also "cut the shoots" like Adam; Noah is called תמים (Gen 6:9) because wholeness flows
  from Chesed. 24: the primordial serpent suckles from Pachad by way of Hod (הו'ד uncertain).
- 28–33: Yeri'ah: והנחש האריך זנבו עד שנגע בראובן. After the flood the serpent's power weakened until Ham, father of Canaan, was
  born (Gen 9:22); ראובן = Malkhut because sight (ראייה) belongs to her; the filth reached "the lower Reuben". Then בלבל יצועי
  אביו ולא חטא ← Gen 49:4 / Shabbat 55b ("whoever says Reuben sinned is mistaken"). Two interlinear additions: הדור (29), כאשר (30).
- 34–41: Gen 47:31 וישתחו ישראל על ראש המטה — the bed = Shekhinah; Joseph refused his mistress (Gen 39). Isa 57:1 הצדיק אבד
  ואין איש שם על לב (red-underlined in 37, quoted again in 41). Yeri'ah: ונקצצו רגלי הנחש (Gen 3:14 midrash, Bereshit Rabbah 20:5);
  ובאו בעגל הזהב על יד יוסף הצדיק — the calf's sin is traced to Joseph's sale (ומכרו אותו לישמעאלים, Gen 37:28). Last lemma
  והציצו ... ונפגעו ← Chagigah 14b (בן זומא הציץ ונפגע); continues on 78b.
- Red underlines that fall between two lines were assigned to the upper line (they sit ~60–75 px below its baseline in the 2x crops).
