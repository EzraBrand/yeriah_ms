## Notes
- Kraken 01–03 are not main-block rows (probably a margin gloss `של נעלך · פי' רמז לדבר ... שנועל ... בכחות שכליו`);
  from kraken 04 on, kraken N = Main_(N-3). Main_44 is a row of filler strokes between 43 and 45 and carries no text.
- 1–5: Flood, Gen 7:20–22. **אמה** (not אמות): חמש עשרה אמה = י"ה; had he said אמות the meaning would be the Atarah
  (ע') included in that Name. **ויכסו ההרים** ← Gen 7:20: the mountains = the upper world, covered by the splendour (זוהר)
  likened to water. **ויגוע** ← Gen 7:21; **ויגוע ויאסף** = the death of the righteous (Gen 25:8 ויגוע … ויאסף) — a superior
  death (מיתה מעולה[?]). כל אשר נשמת רוח חיים באפיו ← Gen 7:22, emanated from Binah (cf. Job 32:8 ונשמת שדי תבינם, line 6),
  so those souls rose to their place; other animals' spirit comes from outside the Chariot and does not endure.
- 8–9: **בחרבה** ← Gen 7:22 (מכל אשר בחרבה מתו): חרבה → חורבן ומלחמות; **מיתה** rolls down to all who come into the world.
- 10–12: **וישאר אך נח** ← Gen 7:23 … up to **גמול ידיו יעשה** (← Isa 3:11). Noah alone = the Righteous (Yesod), the seventh;
  וכל אשר אתו בתבה ← Gen 7:23 (no bar visible); the ark and Noah are one meaning.
- 13–15: the hidden light (אור הגנוז) hidden because two kings cannot use one crown (Chullin 60b); revealed in the ⟨seventh⟩
  millennium (השביעי written above a struck word, probably השלישי).
- 16–18: **ויזכר ﭏהים את נח** ← Gen 8:1; ויזכר from זכר / ה' זכרנו (Ps 115:12?) = male, upper world; ﭏהים = the Atarah (ע');
  they are united in mercy. **כל החיה** ← Gen 8:1.
- 19–23: **הבהמה ההולכת על ארבע** (Yeri'ah after Gen 8:1 / Lev 11:20?): the wild beast (חיה) has more divine flow than
  cattle, the four elements less coarse in it; the birds (העופות; the barred word הלטין[?] is unresolved) are closer to man.
- 24–26: **ובעמק יהושפט** ← Joel 4:2,12; **הוא עמק המלך** ← Gen 14:17 = קו האמצעי (Tiferet); each of the lower species
  receives according to its deeds (כפי מעלליו, cf. Jer 17:10) and **גמול ידיו יעשה להם** ← Isa 3:11.
- 26–29: **והמים** … הלוך וחסור ← Gen 8:3–5; **ומים מרים לא תשתה** (Yeri'ah; cf. Num 5:18 מי המרים); bitter waters = the
  great judgement (דין הגדול), which diminishes as the channel (הצינור[?]) rises to Thought (המחשבה = Keter), the tenth:
  **והעשירי יהיה קדש ליי** ← Lev 27:32. Line 28 **ען יעשהו**[?] unresolved.
- 30–36: all the הויות are holy; therefore Moses was told **של נעלך** ← Ex 3:5 and **ולא יבער הסנה** (Yeri'ah after Ex 3:2):
  nothing above is compelled or changes; change comes from below. נעלך = the material powers; the bush = the middah (מה')
  which turns to mercy for the sake of the lower ones and rules over the serpent; **ויהי למטה בכפך** ← Ex 4:4 (בכפו):
  accusers become defenders, the ע' hinted in the staff is a shield.
- 37–46: **והיד שבה כבשר** ← Ex 4:7: the hand = Miriam = the moon (הלב'[?] = הלבנה), leprous as snow = hardness of judgement.
  **ולא תשוב מי היאור לדם** (Yeri'ah after Ex 4:9 / 7:17–21): the river = מה' (Malkhut/middah); if it were a hard middah it
  would be אש אוכלה (Deut 4:24) burning the אישים = princes of the nations (no nation falls before its prince above falls);
  **כי לא יוכלו לשתות מצרים מימי היאור** ← Ex 7:18,21,24 — no share in the house of God; **והדגה אשר ביאור תמות** and
  **ובאש** ← Ex 7:18: the fish = princes of the nations who receive …

  מה' = מדה/מלכות (34, 39–41), הלב' = הלבנה (38?).
