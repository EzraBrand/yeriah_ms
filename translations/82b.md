## Notes
[^1]: The opening completes 82a:44. Ecclesiastes 1:9 supplies “nothing new under the sun”; the flame in the ember recalls Sefer Yetzirah 1:7 (numbering varies by recension). The commentary locates multiplicity in recipients rather than the divine grades. The abbreviation המ׳ is not expanded. Song of Songs 6:9 supplies “One is my dove.” The masculine “he” after the feminine “she” follows the source. *Bad* means linen and also participates in the language of aloneness or singularity; the next line associates the line with the letter *vav*. {source: Biblical, textual, and lexical note.}
[^2]: Leviticus 16:23 supplies leaving the linen garments, and 16:13 the incense cloud covering the ark-cover so the priest does not die. JTS’s כי יד יי שמה is retained as “the hand of the LORD is there,” unlike Oxford’s shorter כי ייי׳ שמה. The abbreviation מה׳ is provisionally expanded as *Malkhut* throughout this passage; ש׳ as *Shekhinah*, and קו׳ as “line.” These expansions do not establish that each abbreviation has only one possible referent. The unclear ש׳ at line 6 and מד׳ at line 7 are left unexpanded. No numerical value for *vav* is asserted by the translation. {source: Biblical, witness, and abbreviation note.}
[^3]: Exodus 33:20 supplies “no human shall see me and live.” The exposition connects the bodily crown exposed in circumcision with the feminine divine figure; “crown” therefore remains a literal rendering of עטרה, rather than being replaced by *Keter*. “Head of the corner” echoes Psalm 118:22, and “his kingship rules over all” Psalm 103:19. Line 12 moves between masculine and feminine reference; the translation retains this rather than assigning every pronoun to one subject. {source: Biblical and interpretive note.}
[^4]: Genesis 17:12 prescribes circumcision on the eighth day; the commentary situates it above the Binding of Isaac through the relation between *Chesed* and *Pachad*. Its eighth-grade count is preserved as stated. “The line in general and in particular” is not expanded into an invented numerical demonstration. No gematria calculation is asserted. {source: Biblical and numerical note.}
