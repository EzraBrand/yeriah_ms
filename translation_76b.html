Editorial statement. This is a provisional academic translation of the current draft transcription. It follows the manuscript lineation and does not silently emend uncertain or corrupt readings. Technical names are transliterated; “hypostasis” translates הויה where the author appears to mean an emanated divine grade.

Dotted underline marks an uncertain source reading or translation; bold maroon type marks a manuscript lemma. Lines 51–52 remain unread in the source transcription.

Notes

  1. The lemmata combine Ps. 61:4, מגדל עוז (“a tower of strength”), and Ps. 119:80, יהי לבי תמים (“may my heart be blameless”). Their initial letters, מ״י, have the numerical value fifty, here linked to the fifty gates of Binah. Adapted from the transcription notes.↩
  2. “A tower of strength is the name of YHWH” quotes Prov. 18:10. The reading ספר יצי׳ probably abbreviates Sefer Yetzirah, but the attribution and exact reference require verification. Source identification inherited; qualification added.↩
  3. Prov. 18:10 continues, “into it the righteous man runs”; Ps. 91:14 supplies “I shall raise him on high, for he knows My name.” “Fasten their end in their beginning” echoes Sefer Yetzirah 1:7 in common enumerations. Biblical references inherited; Sefer Yetzirah parallel added.↩
  4. Gen. 2:25 reads, “the two of them were naked … and were not ashamed.” The damaged clause in line 15 may connect sight, shame, and the final divine grade (Mah, apparently Malkhut), but the underlying transcription is too uncertain to normalize. Biblical reference inherited; caution added.↩
